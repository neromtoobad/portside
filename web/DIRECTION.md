# Portside visual direction

**Metaphor: the harbour master's office.** A split-flap arrivals board announces the news, a tide gauge counts down the round, every good flies its maritime signal flag, and trades land in a ledger.

## Principles borrowed (not copied)
- From split-flap boards (e.g. [Vestaboard](https://www.vestaboard.com)): one dark, tactile board per screen carries the news. Everything else stays quiet and light. Restrained Inter body text with slight negative tracking.
- From light trading apps: the price is the hero number, colour only means up or down, and figures use tabular numerals.
- From game UIs (lessons from NAND Arena): ink outlines with a hard offset shadow so buttons feel pressable, and a display face with character. No cartoon scenery and no confetti, which the user called "childish" on Loft.

## Tokens
- Canvas `#f3efe6` chart paper with faint depth-contour lines (inline SVG); cards `#fffdf8`
- Ink `#13283f` (navy), muted `#5d6b7a`, rule `#d9d1c1`
- Board `#10202f` with cream flaps `#f1e7d0`
- Up `#1f7a4d`, down `#c4412f` (signal red), brass accent `#b8892f`
- Display: Big Shoulders Display 800 (port-stencil feel). Body: Inter. Figures: JetBrains Mono, tabular.
- Radius 14 (cards) / 10 (buttons). Border 1.5px ink. Shadow `3px 3px 0 #13283f` on actionable things only.
